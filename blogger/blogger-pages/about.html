About Wildlife Universe

Stories, science and conservation from the wild side of our planet.

Who we are

Wildlife Universe is an independent online publication dedicated to the animals, plants, birds and insects that share our planet. From the savannas of East Africa to backyard hedgerows, we tell the stories of species great and small — how they live, why they matter, and what we can do to keep them thriving.

Our writers and researchers turn peer-reviewed science, field reports and conservation data into articles that anyone can enjoy. Every piece is fact-checked against reputable sources before it is published.

Our pillars

Wonder

We celebrate the beauty and strangeness of the natural world, one species at a time.

Accuracy

Every article is grounded in reputable science and reviewed under our fact-checking policy.

Conservation

We highlight IUCN Red List statuses and the people working to protect threatened species.

Community

Comments, reactions and reader questions shape what we explore next.

What we cover

Our library is organised into five categories — Animals, Plants, Birds, Insects and Posts (how & why questions, tourism, conservation and feature articles). We also maintain dedicated coverage of the IUCN Red List.

Editorial independence

Wildlife Universe is reader-first. Advertising never influences our editorial judgements — see our Editorial Policy and Fact-Checking Policy for how we work.
