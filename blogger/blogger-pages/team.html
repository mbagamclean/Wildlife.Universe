Who makes Wildlife Universe

Wildlife Universe is produced by a small, dedicated editorial team supported by contributing writers, photographers and subject-matter reviewers around the world.

Editorial

Commissioning, writing and editing every story to our editorial and fact-checking standards.

Research & fact-checking

Verifying species data, IUCN statuses and conservation claims against primary sources.

Field contributors

Guides, rangers and naturalists who share first-hand observations from the wild.

Community

Moderating discussions and turning reader questions into future articles.
